Choose your name and the first team to evaluate. After that, teams follow in order until all eight are done — you cannot skip ahead.

How scoring works

  • Strict team order — e.g. start with Team 3, then 4→8, then 1→2. You must finish all eight for your name.
  • 5 weighted criteria (0–10 each) with a live weighted total.
  • Overall feedback and one award nomination per team.
  • Local-first via localStorage; structured so you can later swap to Supabase.
